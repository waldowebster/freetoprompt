## Listing Draft

Subtitle: Offline Teleprompter

Google Play short description: A free offline teleprompter with adjustable speed, mirroring, and local saving.

Description:

Free to Prompt puts your words at your pace.
Write or import a script, adjust the text and scrolling speed, and start prompting.

- Smooth scrolling with adjustable speed, text size, brightness, and width.
- Horizontal mirroring and vertical flipping for teleprompter hardware.
- Offline operation with automatic local saving.
- Plain-text import and export through your device's share sheet.
- Touch scrolling and keyboard controls.
- Phone and tablet layouts in portrait and landscape.

No signup, subscriptions, ads, or tracking.
Your scripts stay on your device unless you choose to export them.
Free to Prompt is open source under the MIT license, so you can customize it yourself.

## Review Notes Draft

No account, login, subscription, or purchase is required.
The app includes a welcome script for immediate testing.
Tap Play to start prompting, Pause to stop scrolling, and Edit Script to return.
Mirror and Flip support physical teleprompter rigs.
All core functionality works offline.
Export opens the operating system's share sheet with a plain-text file.
