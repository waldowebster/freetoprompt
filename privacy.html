Privacy Policy

Free to Prompt is provided by Waldough, Inc.

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

Your Scripts and Settings

The app processes scripts on your device. The browser version saves your current script and settings in browser storage. The installed mobile apps save them in private app storage. Free to Prompt does not send your scripts or settings to Waldough, Inc.

Free to Prompt has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Importing and Exporting

Import reads only the file you select. Export creates a plain-text copy. In the mobile apps, a temporary copy is stored in the app cache and shared only through the destination you choose in the system share sheet. That destination handles the copy under its own privacy policy.

Deleting Your Data

Reset clears the current saved script. Clearing browser site data or deleting the mobile app removes the app's local data, subject to your operating system's backup and restore settings. Exported copies and device backups must be managed separately.

Website and External Links

When you visit freetoprompt.com, the hosting provider processes normal connection information, such as your IP address, to deliver the site. Following links to GitHub, waldo.biz, or another service is subject to that service's privacy policy.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include to respond to your request. Support mail is forwarded through ImprovMX to our support inbox. Please avoid including private scripts or sensitive information unless necessary for your request.

For support or privacy questions, contact support@freetoprompt.com.
